Turn scattered notes into editable action drafts. Check each draft beside its original words, then take your list away as Markdown.

Inside the walkthrough

  1. Paste a synthetic project handoff and select Find actions.
  2. Review three drafts and their original quotations. Mina's checklist task keeps “If the review passes.”
  3. Edit a draft and mark it done. The original evidence stays attached.
  4. Download the action list as Markdown.

An open model, a small chore

The demo runs Qwen locally on an Apple silicon Mac. The app is MIT licensed; model weights are downloaded separately under their own licenses.

Optional Groq processing must be selected explicitly. It sends submitted notes to Groq and may use paid API credit. Fresh pages default to local processing.

Processing and privacy details

Review the source, even when the list looks finished.

Long meetings remain a known weakness. The latest recorded hosted test found nine of ten expected actions in a synthetic 120-turn meeting and failed acceptance. Source checks help you inspect a draft; they do not establish completeness or correct interpretation.

Built and documented with AI agents. The app does not send messages, schedule tasks or execute actions. Demo inputs are synthetic; no private meeting is published here.
