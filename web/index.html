    How this works

    Your notes go to a model running on this machine. It suggests actions and attaches a complete source line to each one, keeping its conditions in view. Source lines must match your notes; suggestions can still be wrong. Put each action and its conditions together on one line for clearer drafts. Edit, tick off or remove any action, then export your list. Refreshing or closing this tab clears your work.
