    How it works & privacy

    Your text goes to a model running on this machine. For notes, keep each action and its conditions on one line. Transcripts with speaker names and timestamps are recognized automatically; keep the full conversation so qualifications remain available. Drafts retain original source text and nearby transcript context. Source matching does not prove that an action, owner or condition is correct, or that every action was found. Remove sensitive information before sharing an export. Refreshing or closing this tab clears your work.
